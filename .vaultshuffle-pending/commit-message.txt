Fit the draw and the pick on one screen, and bring Recent activity back

Recent activity was empty, and that was my doing. The row filtered on the
sort key being positive, from when that key was a timestamp; I changed it to
a negated day count, which is negative for every game that has ever been
played. It now filters on having recency evidence at all, which is what the
test was always trying to say.

The result card was too tall to see alongside the draw bar, so the case it
makes for a game - the whole point of the card - sat below the fold. It is
601px down to 495px, and 767px including the bar and the offset above it even
with a full four-reason explanation, inside an 830px viewport.

Most of that came from parts that were not earning their space. The artwork
is a banner rather than a feature. The setup summary along the bottom went
from 95px to 52px by sitting its labels and values on one line - it repeats
choices the player made moments ago and can still see in the bar above, which
is worth keeping but not worth a sixth of the card. The description clamps to
two lines, which is enough of a Steam synopsis to recognise a game by. And
the "Vault actions" heading is gone: three buttons filled in the colour of
what they do do not need to be told what they are.
